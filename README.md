# Angel Buddy
AI-powered assistant built for beginner entrepreneurs to guide them through key business areas.
## Problem Statement
Beginner entrepreneurs often feel overwhelmed managing their business alone. They lack access to affordable mentorship and struggle with motivation and staying consistent, especially when it comes to managing people — one of the biggest challenges for small businesses according to business advisors.
## Solution
Angel Buddy is a warm, guided AI assistant that helps entrepreneurs navigate five key business areas: Marketing, Finance, Sales, Operations, and People/HR. Instead of overwhelming users with information, it asks guiding questions and gives concrete, actionable advice, like a real mentor would.
## AI Approach and Architecture
Angel Buddy was built using IBM Bob as the primary AI development tool. Bob generated the full frontend (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), including the guided chat logic and content structure. The chat provides contextual, keyword-driven responses tailored to each business area.
## Challenge Theme
July Challenge — Reimagine Creative Industries with AI
## How IBM Bob Was Used
IBM Bob was used to design and build the entire application: the page structure, styling, and the interactive chat logic. Bob also diagnosed and fixed bugs during development (such as a modal click event conflict) and iteratively expanded the chat responses for each business area.

Angel Buddy — AI Business Companion for Beginner Entrepreneurs
Overview
Angel Buddy is a Spanish-language AI business companion designed to help beginner entrepreneurs take their first steps with more confidence.
Starting a business requires knowledge in many different areas, including marketing, finance, sales, operations, and human resources. However, many new entrepreneurs do not know where to find reliable information or how to apply it to their own situation.
Angel Buddy brings essential business guidance and learning resources together in one simple and friendly platform.
The Problem
Many people have great business ideas but struggle when turning them into reality because they lack practical knowledge about running a business.
Common challenges include:
How to promote a new business.
How to understand basic financial decisions.
How to attract and retain customers.
How to organize business operations.
How to manage people and teams.
Current resources are often scattered, too complex, or not adapted for someone who is just starting.
Our Solution
Angel Buddy acts as a digital companion for early-stage entrepreneurs.
The platform provides guidance across five key business areas:
Marketing — understanding customers, branding, and promotion strategies.
Finance — learning basic financial concepts and decision-making.
Sales — improving customer acquisition and sales processes.
Operations — organizing processes and improving efficiency.
People & HR — understanding team management and human resources.
It also includes a Resources & News section, where entrepreneurs can discover useful business content and continue developing their knowledge.
